## Immediate plan in simple terms

A person asks for help funding a purchase. Another person reviews the request.
They agree on the same version. The application uses XRPL to make and repay a
test loan. Recognitium supplies receipts linking their agreement to those results.
An AI can help find and prepare the request, while the people approve the money
movement. A judge can follow the records and check a changed document fails.

The event judges both the working result and what developers learn while
building it. A later pilot would let an external developer try the same workflow
and measure whether the integration and evidence are useful.
